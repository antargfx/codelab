WHAT HAPPENS LOCALLY

01

Images are rebuilt

Supported images are decoded and re-encoded through the browser canvas. This typically removes embedded metadata, but can change image quality, transparency, color profiles, animation, or other format features.

02

Videos are re-encoded

FFmpeg.wasm attempts to decode and re-encode video/audio and drops ordinary container metadata. This is resource-intensive and can fail for unsupported codecs or large files.

03

Results are verified honestly

The report distinguishes successful processing from unsupported files and errors. This page does not independently certify that EXIF, XMP, IPTC, C2PA, or every other trace is absent.
